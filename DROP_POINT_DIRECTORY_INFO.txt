CEBU FARE GUIDE V5 — DROP POINT DIRECTORY

Loaded 844 selectable entries from the previously supplied Cebu drop-point directory, plus route stops and LGU names.

Coordinates are not fabricated for every entry. Known starter points keep their checked coordinates; other entries are geocoded on demand using OpenStreetMap Nominatim when selected or when calculating a route. If a place cannot be geocoded, use Pin Origin / Pin Destination to set its exact position manually.

Route codes and corridors transcribed from the user's message are marked unverified. Unknown codes remain listed with blank alignment until confirmed. This is not an official route inventory.
