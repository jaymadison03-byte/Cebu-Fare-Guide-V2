V26 UPDATE

Passengers now see a single “Jeepney / Multicab” category instead of separate Traditional Jeepney and Multicab categories. The public route finder filter, route cards, automatic journey results, and fare-estimate selector use the combined label.

Internal route classifications are intentionally retained so the coverage rules continue to work correctly. Traditional jeepney routes remain Cebu City-only. Multicab routes remain limited to Talisay–Carcar in the south and Mandaue–Danao in the north.
